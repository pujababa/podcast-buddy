        Podcast Buddy — System Architecture

        An enterprise-grade, full-stack AI audio assistant transforming live global news reporting into grounded, conversational multi-speaker podcasts.

        🔍

        Live News Aggregation

        SerpAPI integration fetching real-time verified articles, with keyword filtering, categories, and direct publisher traceability.

        🧠

        Grounded AI Intelligence

        Google Gemini extracts structured overviews, key takeaways, named entities, and confidence scores strictly from verified source snippets.

        🎙️

        Two-Speaker Dialogue Scripting

        Automated conversational scripting between Host and CoHost, organizing news digests into introductions, thematic story segments, and outro recaps.

        🔊

        Regional Neural Speech Synthesis

        High-fidelity dual-speaker neural speech across Odia (Sarvam AI Bulbul v3), Hindi (Edge Neural Madhur & Swara), Hinglish, and English (Guy & Jenny) with 250ms conversational pauses and loudness normalization.
